What is the difference between exact deduplication and fuzzy record linkage?	"<b>English:</b> Exact deduplication matches identical primary keys or hash values. Record linkage compares string similarities (Levenshtein/Jaro-Winkler) across non-identical candidate pairs to link entities without shared foreign keys.<br><br><b>Portuguese:</b> Deduplicação exata busca registros 100% idênticos. O Record Linkage usa similaridade de texto (Levenshtein/Jaro-Winkler) para unir registros semelhantes sem chave estrangeira comum."
What does pd.to_datetime(..., errors='coerce') do when it encounters an invalid date string?	"<b>English:</b> It converts unparseable or corrupted date values into NaT (Not a Time / null) instead of crashing the pipeline with a ValueError.<br><br><b>Portuguese:</b> Converte datas corrompidas ou inválidas em NaT (Not a Time / nulo), impedindo a quebra do pipeline com ValueError."
What is 'blocking' in the record linkage workflow and why is it critical for Big Data?	"<b>English:</b> Blocking subsets candidate comparisons by grouping on a reliable common feature (such as postal code), reducing the comparison space from quadratic O(N * M) to manageable linear blocks.<br><br><b>Portuguese:</b> Agrupa candidatos por um atributo comum (ex: CEP), reduzindo o espaço de comparações de O(N * M) quadrático para blocos lineares tratáveis."
How does the drop_duplicates(subset=[...], keep='last') method work?	"<b>English:</b> It identifies rows that share identical values across the specified subset columns and retains only the final occurrence, dropping all previous duplicates.<br><br><b>Portuguese:</b> Identifica linhas com valores idênticos nas colunas especificadas no subset e preserva apenas a última ocorrência encontrada."
Why is defensive assertion (assert df['col'].notnull().all()) valuable in pipeline stages?	"<b>English:</b> It acts as a circuit breaker, halting pipeline propagation immediately when corrupt data violates business invariants rather than polluting downstream data warehouses.<br><br><b>Portuguese:</b> Atua como disjuntor (circuit breaker), interrompendo o pipeline imediatamente se dados corrompidos violarem regras de negócio antes de poluir o data warehouse."
